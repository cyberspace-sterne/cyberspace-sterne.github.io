This website is currently under construction as the future home of Laurence Sterne in Cyberspace (https://www1.gifu-u.ac.jp/~masaru/Sterne_on_the_Net.html) and Tristram Shandy Online(https://www1.gifu-u.ac.jp/~masaru/TS/contents.html), websites maintained by Masaru Uchida, Professor of English Literature at Gifu University. The transfer is scheduled for April 2028.
